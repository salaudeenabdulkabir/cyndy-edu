WEBSITE INFORMATION

Website privacy

Updated 19 September 2026. This notice covers the public information website operated by Cyndy Educational Pathways.

Browsing this website

This website has no account registration, document uploads, payment forms or advertising trackers. We have not added analytics or tracking cookies.

Cloudflare hosts the website and may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request details, to deliver and protect it. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond to your enquiry. Our contact inbox is hosted by Google. Please send only the information needed for your question, and do not send identity documents or financial records.

Questions and requests

To ask about your information or request correction or deletion of an enquiry, contact cyndyeducationalpathways7@gmail.com.

Future application services

The online applicant portal is not available on this website. Its privacy information and service terms will be provided before applications open.
